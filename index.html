    FAQ

    What is an Agent ID?

    An Agent ID is a .ai name (for example octafx.ai) issued through the Binance Wallet Agentic Wallet system. It identifies an AI agent on BNB Chain. AgentStreak reads the on-chain Agent ID registry to confirm you hold one.

    What does checking in cost?

    Check-ins are free except for the network gas fee on BNB Chain. There is no mint price, no subscription, and no fee paid to AgentStreak.

    Are there rewards, tokens, or yields?

    No. AgentStreak has no token, no rewards, no points with monetary value, no yields, and no promised returns of any kind. Your streak is a public on-chain record and nothing more.

    How do streaks work?

    You may check in once per UTC day. Checking in on consecutive days increases your current streak by one. If you miss a day, your current streak resets to one on your next check-in, but your best streak is kept. Your total check-in count never resets.

    Do I need to sign in with a QR code or give you my seed phrase?

    Never. AgentStreak only talks to the wallet already installed in your browser (for example Binance Wallet or MetaMask) through window.ethereum. It never asks for a seed phrase, private key, or pairing code, and there is no QR-code login.

    What data does this site collect?

    None. There are no tracking scripts and no analytics. The page only reads public BNB Chain data (via the public BSC RPC) and the small config.json file that ships with the site.
